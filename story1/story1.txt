Rocco is a clever young raccoon. He lives in a big, noisy town. Rocco has a very special talent. He can paint beautiful pictures with his small paws. His pictures look bright and happy. But Rocco does not like to work hard for a long time. He wants fast money today. He wants to buy sweet cakes and soft hats without waiting.

One morning, a fat fox walks by the road. His name is Barnaby. Barnaby looks at Rocco and laughs. Barnaby says, "Hey, little raccoon, do you want fast money?" Rocco grins and says, "Yes, I want fast money right now!" Barnaby gives him two shiny copper coins. For these two coins, Rocco must wear a giant yellow banana suit. He must stand in front of a soup shop and wave his arms. The banana suit is heavy and hot. Many people point at Rocco. They laugh out loud. Rocco feels silly, but he holds his two coins and smiles.

The next morning, a big badger needs a worker. The badger says, "Carry these heavy bags of smelly trash for three coins!" Rocco says, "I can do that fast!" He picks up the big bags. The trash is wet and old. Black dirt gets on his face and fur. Rocco smells bad. His old friends, Leo the rabbit and Mia the owl, walk down the street. Leo and Mia stop and look at Rocco. Leo says, "Rocco, you are a great artist. Why are you carrying smelly trash for a few coins?" Rocco shakes his head and says, "I do not care about art, Leo! I have three coins in my pocket today!" Leo and Mia look sad. They turn around and walk away.

On the third morning, a tall giraffe comes to the square. The giraffe says, "I need a hat, but I am too tall for normal hats! I will give you four coins if you stand on my head all day like a hat!" Rocco says, "Four coins is good money!" He climbs up a high tree and jumps onto the giraffe's head. Rocco sits there under the hot sun for six hours. Birds fly around his face. A bird tries to make a nest on Rocco's fur. People take funny photos with their phones. Rocco gets very tired and hot.

On the fourth morning, a silly frog comes to the park. The frog says, "I will pay you five coins if you jump like a frog in the cold pond!" Rocco says, "Five coins is a big prize!" He jumps into the cold green water. He makes loud frog noises. Splash! Splash! Many animals gather near the water. They throw popcorn at Rocco. They laugh at the silly wet raccoon. Rocco gets very cold. His fur is soaked and heavy. He catches a cold and sneezes many times, but he collects his five small coins.

On the fifth morning, a rich wolf walks into the market. The wolf says, "I need a living footstool for my big boots! I will pay you six coins if you lie on the cold stone floor!" Rocco lies down on the hard stones. The rich wolf puts his heavy boots on Rocco's back. The boots are dirty and full of small rocks. Rocco's back hurts a lot. He cannot move for two long hours. A big dog walks past and spills cold water on Rocco's head. Everyone in the market laughs at the living footstool.

On the sixth morning, a loud rooster comes with a drum. The rooster says, "I will give you seven coins if you let people throw soft tomatoes at your face!" Rocco says, "Okay, seven coins is good!" He stands near a wooden wall. Many animals buy red tomatoes. They throw the tomatoes at Rocco's face and chest. Red tomato juice goes into his eyes and ears. The crowds cheer and laugh loudly. Rocco feels small and empty inside. His fur is red and sticky.

That night, Rocco walks home very slowly. He counts his small pile of coins on the table. He has twenty-seven small coins. But his back hurts. His head hurts. He smells like old tomatoes, pond water, and smelly trash. He has no friends left. He loses his good name for a few cheap coins. Rocco looks in the mirror and starts to cry. He says to himself, "This easy money costs too much. I am not a banana. I am not a footstool. I am an artist."

The next day, Rocco makes a big decision. He says, "I will not do silly jobs anymore!" He locks his front door. He washes his dirty fur with warm water and soap. He cleans his small room. Then, he picks up his paint and brushes. He puts his small paws into bright blue, yellow, and red paint. He paints all day and all night. He paints trees, stars, rivers, and mountains. He paints for seven long days without stopping. He puts his heart into every picture.

After one week, Rocco takes five of his best paintings. He walks to the central town market. He sets up a small wooden stall in a quiet corner. He places his pictures on the table. The sun shines on the beautiful colors. Rocco sits behind his table, tall and straight. He looks proud and clean.

A rich bear walks past the stall. She wears a golden coat and black glasses. She owns the biggest picture gallery in the big city. The bear stops in front of Rocco's stall. She looks at one painting of a blue forest. Her eyes open wide. The bear says, "This picture is very beautiful. I will buy it from you for ten small coins."

Rocco looks at the bear. He remembers the tall giraffe, the smelly trash, and the red tomatoes. He knows his work is good now. Rocco says, "No, thank you. Ten coins is too low. My art takes time, hard work, and love. The price for this painting is two hundred gold coins."

The rich bear looks surprised. Then she smiles warmly. The bear says, "You are right, young raccoon. Real talent is valuable. I respect an artist who respects his own work. I will give you two hundred gold coins for this picture, and I want to buy four more!" She pays Rocco a big bag of heavy gold coins.

Leo the rabbit and Mia the owl see everything from across the street. They see Rocco standing tall and proud with his real art. They walk over to his stall with big smiles on their faces. Mia says, "Hello, Rocco. Your paintings are wonderful. We are very happy for you." Rocco smiles at his old friends and says, "Thank you. My time is finally worth something real."
